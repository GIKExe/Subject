\noindent \textbf{Текстовое описание от VLM} \\
\indent [Subject]: \\
A severely burnt and melted Corsair CX600M power supply unit (PSU) as the central focus. The black modular cables are charred, fused together, and bubbling from extreme heat, spilling out from the right side of the unit towards the left. The metal casing is covered in thick black soot and bubbling heat damage. The "CX600M" green and white label is visible on the right side of the unit. The composition is non-mirrored: PSU is on the right, cables extend to the left. \\
\indent [Environment]: \\
A cluttered electronics repair workbench. In the background, a wooden pegboard is mounted with neatly organized hand tools (screwdrivers with yellow/black handles, various pliers). On the green cutting mat surface, there are scattered black debris, screws, a soldering station on the left, and rolls of solder. The background is slightly out of focus (shallow depth of field). \\
\indent [Style]: \\
Hyper-realistic macro photography, gritty industrial aesthetic, cinematic close-up. High level of detail on textures like melted plastic, soot, and brushed metal. \\
\indent [Lighting]: \\
Focused, warm overhead task lighting from a black adjustable desk lamp. Intense highlights on the top of the PSU and the melted cables, creating deep, dramatic shadows (chiaroscuro effect). The light source is visible at the top center. \\
\indent [Color Palette]: \\
Dominant shades of charcoal black, soot gray, and metallic silver. Accents of "Corsair green" on the label, yellow/red on tool handles, and a warm amber glow from the lamp contrasting with the cool teal of the cutting mat. \\
